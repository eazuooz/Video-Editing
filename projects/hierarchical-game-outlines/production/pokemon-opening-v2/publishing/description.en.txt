Why does a long list of design notes need levels? Start with an illustrative document based on early Pokémon, grouping Exploration, Battles, and Item Use so that related rules can be found together.

Then use Super Smash Bros. Ultimate menus and move descriptions to distinguish collapsing, moving, and changing levels, before preparing a document for another reader. The planning document is our own teaching example, not an original developer document. Gameplay and menu operations come from independently obtained recordings.
