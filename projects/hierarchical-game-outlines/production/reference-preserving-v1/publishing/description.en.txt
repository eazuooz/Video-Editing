Turn a long list of notes into a structure you can navigate and revise. We distinguish collapsing a branch, moving it with its children, and changing its parent.

Following the progression of Masahiro Sakurai’s lesson on hierarchical outlines, this explanation uses independently obtained Super Smash Bros. Ultimate footage and our own diagrams and narration. The menu is an analogy for grouping text, not a claim about the game’s internal code or Nintendo’s design documents.
