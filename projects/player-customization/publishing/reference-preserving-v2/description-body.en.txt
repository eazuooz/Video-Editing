Why can choosing equipment and styling a character be as enjoyable as playing? We begin with the visible weapon previews in Jets’n’Guns 2, then explore part trade-offs in Armored Core VI and the categories and icons used in The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild and Monster Hunter World: Iceborne.

Readable choices lead into TerraTech’s short building-and-testing loop, followed by personal taste and expression in Animal Crossing: New Horizons. Consider not only what players can change, but how they choose, check the result, and revise it.

The fictional weapon calculations and the sorting and auto-equip menus are independently authored illustrations. They do not reproduce a specific game’s auto-equip feature or establish an optimal setup for every situation. The Armored Core VI values are displayed menu comparisons, not measured movement speeds.
