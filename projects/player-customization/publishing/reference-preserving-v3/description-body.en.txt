Why can choosing equipment and styling a character be as enjoyable as playing? We begin with selecting equipment and checking its result on the test range in Splatoon Raiders, then consider how clear trade-offs create meaningful choices.

We connect the categories, icons, and quick weapon selection in The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild with the appearance previews in Animal Crossing: New Horizons. Finding an option, trying it, and revising it to suit your taste can be part of the game’s enjoyment.

The fictional weapon calculations and the sorting and auto-equip menus are independently authored illustrations. They do not reproduce these games’ actual statistics or auto-equip features, or establish an optimal setup for every situation.
