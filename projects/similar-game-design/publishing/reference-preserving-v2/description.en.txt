Why would someone choose a similar new game when a game they already enjoy exists?
We look at mining and extraction in Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor, then world creation and multiplayer racing in Super Mario Maker 2. See how a new purpose, role, or relationship changes the choices made with familiar controls.
Starting with a game you love, finishing something playable, and shaping several appeals into one distinctive experience: that is the game-design problem explored here.
