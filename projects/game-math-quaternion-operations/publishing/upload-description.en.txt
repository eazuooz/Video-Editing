How can four numbers record an airborne rotation in a game? We will observe ski and bike orientations, then visualize the four quaternion components and their half-angle. We will undo and compose rotations, and compute the difference between two orientations. Finally, we will check sign and normalization pitfalls and rotate a vector in a worked exercise.
