How does game water seem to flow around a rock?
Real Left 4 Dead 2 footage and our Unity shader demonstration reveal the direction texture behind the effect: a flow map.

See how RG channels encode direction, how the shader moves texture samples, why two staggered cycles hide the resets, and why moving an obstacle requires updating the map. This is a visual water-flow shader, not a complete fluid simulation.
