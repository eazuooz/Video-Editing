Why do the same angles produce different orientations when their order changes? We will observe flight and distinguish the three Euler rotations and their moving axes. Then we will change the order and examine gimbal lock and angle interpolation. Finally, we will describe a rotation with one axis and angle, and solve two problems about rotation vectors and their limits.
