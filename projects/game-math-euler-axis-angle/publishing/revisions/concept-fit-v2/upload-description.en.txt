Observe a hook being rotated and attached in Tears of the Kingdom and a tile being rotated into place in Dorfromantik. We connect those actions to Euler-axis order, gimbal lock, angular interpolation, axis-angle and rotation vectors.

Game recordings and our mathematical models are labeled separately. Numerical examples are our calculations, not measurements of the games. No background-music track was added.
