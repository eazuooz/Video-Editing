Why can changing the order of rotations change an object's orientation? Follow an aircraft model one axis at a time, then connect Euler-angle conventions, repeated representations, gimbal lock and interpolation to axis–angle and rotation vectors.

Definitions come before diagrams, motion and small worked calculations. Brief Mario Kart, Zelda and Mario recordings identify visible examples; their overlays do not measure internal game rotation values.

Convention: left-handed coordinates, body-axis Y-X-Z order and row vectors. Axis, sign and multiplication conventions must be translated together when using other references.
