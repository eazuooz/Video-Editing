Why can an object tilt while facing the same direction? We will observe flight in a game and distinguish direction from orientation. Then we will build a rotation matrix from three axes and convert local coordinates into world coordinates. Finally, we will check inverse transforms and invalid rotations, then solve two problems to verify the signs and calculation order.

We connect flight in Superflight, bicycle orientation in Descenders, an assembled aircraft in Trailmakers and component placement in Space Engineers to compare the same representation of orientation.
