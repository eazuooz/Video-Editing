Position and orientation carry different information. Starting with object assembly in Tears of the Kingdom and part rotation in Besiege, we record three object axes in a rotation matrix and transform between local and world coordinates. The lesson retains the progression through direction cosines, inverse transforms, orthonormality and implementation.

Actual game recordings and our explanatory models are labeled separately. Model coordinates and calculations are not measurements of internal game state. No background-music track was added.
