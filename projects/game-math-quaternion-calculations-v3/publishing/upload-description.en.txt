Once a rotation is stored in four numbers, in what order should two rotations be composed? We connect the body in Riders Republic, an aircraft in Trailmakers, a part preview in Space Engineers, and barrel directions in Besiege to compare composition, target orientation and vector rotation. We reverse the order of rotations about fixed axes, calculate the change from a current to a target orientation, and rotate the same vector to check direction and length. A single part and an entire aircraft are different targets. Screen annotations do not measure engine internals; calculations use an illustrative model with stated coordinate and multiplication conventions. This second quaternion lecture preserves the existing material and calculations.
