“That's enough for today... Wait, I'm only 300 gold away from that backpack!”
Have you ever been about to quit a game, only to decide to do just one more thing?

This video explores how game design makes rewards and goals visible, using Stardew Valley's backpack upgrades, Vampire Survivors' XP and level-up choices, and Monster Hunter Wilds' equipment crafting menus as examples.

It isn't simply about offering bigger rewards. Players need to understand why they want a reward, how close they are to it, and how it will change the way they play.

Through real gameplay and original 2.5D illustrations, we examine how to connect current progress to a goal, make the benefits of a reward tangible, and show information when it matters. We also explain why “one material left” doesn't necessarily mean “one more hunt.”

For anyone interested in game design, reward systems, or game UX, this isn't about keeping players hooked at all costs. It's about helping them set their own goals and look forward to what comes next. The journey toward a reward should be fun, too.

Which game—and which reward—made you think, “Just one more thing, then I'll stop”? Let us know in the comments!
