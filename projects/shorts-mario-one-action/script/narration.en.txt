The cap that made coins appear now lets you control a dinosaur.
In Super Mario Odyssey, it all starts with throwing your cap.
First, throw your cap around and coins pop out. You learn that the world responds.
Then, use that same action on a Chain Chomp, and this time you control its body.
Borrowing another creature’s body and abilities like this is called Capture.
In our demonstration, the button and the throw stay the same.
Change only the target: an action that earned rewards now unlocks a new ability.
You do not have to learn the cap throw from scratch.
Of course, moving and attacking as a dinosaur still take practice.
Even when Odyssey introduces a new ability, it starts with an action you already know.
