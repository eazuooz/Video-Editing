Do developers build every room behind a city’s windows?
Interior mapping creates room depth on a flat window. A custom Unity demonstration shows the view ray, virtual walls, texture lookup and a comparison with a fixed photograph. SimCity is a documented example of this technique.

The game recording supplies city-window context. The explanatory shader is our own implementation, not a measurement of SimCity’s internal geometry.
