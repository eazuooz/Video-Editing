Even a simple rule—connect four pieces of the same color—can become difficult when the colors are hard to distinguish.
Using Puyo Puyo Tetris 2, Super Mario Bros. Wonder and a controlled Unity scene, we change color, lightness, backgrounds and shape cues one at a time. See how color-vision simulation differs from a grayscale check, and how clearer boundaries guide players toward the path and goal.
