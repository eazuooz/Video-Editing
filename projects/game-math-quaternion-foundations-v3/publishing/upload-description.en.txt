Why do we need four numbers to store a rotation? We begin with imaginary numbers and planar rotation, then connect half angles, normalization and inverses. We compare skier orientation in Riders Republic, aircraft banking in Trailmakers, part-placement directions in Space Engineers, and rotation of selected parts in Besiege. Applying the same principle to different objects, we distinguish position from orientation, one direction from a complete orientation, and undoing a rotation from rewinding a traveled path. This first of two lectures preserves the existing explanations and calculations. Gameplay lines describe visible screen relationships; numerical examples use our explicitly defined models.
