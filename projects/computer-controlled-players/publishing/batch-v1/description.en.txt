Computers can evaluate a situation quickly. But would an opponent that blocks and dodges perfectly be fun?
We examine information, decision-making, perceived fairness, reaction time, and openings through Super Smash Bros. Ultimate and Dicey Dungeons. The spaces and twelve-frame example are explanatory models, not measured game values.
