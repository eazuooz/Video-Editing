Would an opponent that blocks and dodges perfectly actually be fun to fight? Super Smash Bros. Ultimate and Dicey Dungeons illustrate information, decisions, perceived fairness, reaction time and openings a player can exploit.

Original Unity demonstrations show how distance, priorities and response delays change behavior. The two-/three-square distances and 12-frame delay are illustrative values, not measurements of either game.
