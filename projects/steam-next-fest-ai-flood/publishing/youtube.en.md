## Title

AI Made Eight Times the Demos — So Why Don't Players Get Better Games for Less?

## Description (body)



Three years ago Steam Next Fest had about a thousand demos; now it has more than eight thousand. This video asks what those numbers changed for developers, players and the idea-driven indie market — and what to prepare for the next three years.

- Steam's AI disclosure: refusal (2023) → allowed if written down (2024) → only what players see and hear (2026).
- GDC 2026: 36% use generative AI; "negative impact" answers 18% → 30% → 52%.
- Players: 8× the choice, flat prices ($21.8 → $21.4), $70/$80 big releases.
- Indies: ideas get cloned on sight (14+ Needle In A Haystack clones); only 3% of 2025's 20,282 releases passed 1,000 reviews.
- Preparation: AI as a tool, humans own the screen, honest disclosure; execution and people as the moat.

All footage is our own screen recording of Steam and public report/article pages; numbers and quotes come from the sources below.
