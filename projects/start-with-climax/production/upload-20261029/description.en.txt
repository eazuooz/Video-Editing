Why can a game hook us before we know its whole story? The opening battle of DOOM (2016), the actions and threat in Super Mario Galaxy 2, and the first trial in Ace Attorney show different ways to design an opening.

We separate the order of events from the order of information and explore how a first action, first threat and first decision can let players experience the appeal of a game early.
