The loot in Diablo II: what if the grass hides it?

Resurrected's new visuals added physical grass. That grass could also obscure the loot.

Their solution was to move the grass out of the way when an item dropped.

In our illustration, the item’s position pushes only nearby blades outward. The scene stays; the item's outline appears.

Changing vegetation’s position or shape like this is a form of vegetation displacement. The key was what players needed to see.

Adding realistic grass was only part of the job. Preserving the joy of finding loot was part of the visual upgrade.
