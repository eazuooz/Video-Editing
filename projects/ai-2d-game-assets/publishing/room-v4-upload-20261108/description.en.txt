We turn AI-assisted art into a small playable room, keeping the same character and setting throughout. Follow the actual prompts, settings and fixes for pixel cleanup, walking, sitting, map reading and writing with a quill.

Play in your browser — no installation or login:
https://yamyam-cartographers-room.eazuooz.chatgpt.site/
Try five actions, four weather states and a twenty-track CC0 lo-fi radio. Both the video and live game show the foreground desk, in-stage radio and memo features.

Seven featured open-source workflows produced outputs, alongside a ComfyUI comparison. The eighth tool's incomplete reproduction is reported separately. Tool roles, links and execution limits are at the bottom.
