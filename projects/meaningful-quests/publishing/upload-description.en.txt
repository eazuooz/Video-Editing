Why do some fetch quests feel tedious? We examine travel and gathering in Skyrim, resource exploration in Subnautica, and an original playable delivery prototype.

Design the choices along the way, visible consequences after completion, and new information on return. Equal-speed, equal-item, equal-score tests compare actual bridge collision state and return routes. Automated inputs verify mechanics, not enjoyment. Korean narration with manually prepared English subtitles.
