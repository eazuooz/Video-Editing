A long playtime can feel like a burden before purchase and a happy record after the fun begins. Unpacking, Super Smash Bros. Ultimate and PowerWash Simulator illustrate why players choose to spend their time.

The 300 hours in the title is an illustrative assumption, not a completion-time claim about any featured game. We discuss opportunity cost, barriers to starting, and a worked example that preserves 40 seconds of meaningful interaction while reducing unnecessary waiting.
