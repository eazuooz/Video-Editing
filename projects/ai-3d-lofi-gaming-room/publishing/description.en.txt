Build the same playable LOFI room with AI: prepare image inputs, generate and retopologize models in Tripo, then use Codex and Unity to connect walking, reading, music, weather, notes and a focus timer. The tutorial shows requests, settings, outputs and handoffs. Four tools form the demonstrated route; six alternatives are discussed separately.

Detailed guide and reproducible requests

The game is currently in local final review. A public play link will be added after public deployment.
