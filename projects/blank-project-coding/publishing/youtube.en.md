# CS Freshmen and Sophomores: Close the Tutorial and Start from Scratch

You understand a tutorial, but a blank project leaves you stuck at the first line. Try closing the finished solution and rebuilding one small feature yourself.

We work through linked-list nodes, insertion and deletion; falling-block movement, collision, rotation and row clearing; and a small brick-breaking game. The focus is on choosing data and implementation order, inspecting runtime state, and checking expectations against actual input.

The goal is to keep developing your own judgment while using AI and other tools. Choose your practice by what you can independently design and verify, rather than your year of study alone.

The development and gameplay footage shows original educational examples executing in a workbench made for this video.
