The first lecture found the distance to a surface and its closest point. Being on an infinite plane still does not establish that a point lies inside a finite platform. We retain the same triangle and point while moving from area to weighted vertex sums and then reading weights from the given point. Matching projected screen positions also requires a separate coplanarity check. Finally, the same weights transfer surface values such as color at that position. This is the second of two lectures on planes and triangles.
