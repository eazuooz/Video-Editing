How did lighting in games begin? This episode connects DOOM’s sector brightness to Quake lightmaps, normals, shadows, forward and deferred shading, and SSAO.

We combine actual game footage and Unreal Engine development demonstrations with Three.js 3D demonstrations, Manim mathematical animations, and explanatory diagrams. Learn to separate visibility from lighting, identify what is stored in advance, and follow the data read and calculated at runtime. Visible results are distinguished from claims about internal implementation.
