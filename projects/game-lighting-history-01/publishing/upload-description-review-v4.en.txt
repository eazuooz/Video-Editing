What do games store, and what do they calculate to create light and shadows? Follow DOOM sector lighting, Quake lightmaps, normals and shadows, then Forward and Deferred Rendering.

Annotations identify what to observe in gameplay and engine demonstrations. Spatial explanations and small numerical examples connect those observations to the calculations. Overlay marks and illustrative numbers are teaching aids, not measured internal game-engine values.
