Why do we sometimes want to skip a beautiful cutscene? Dialogue in Hades II and Animal Crossing: New Horizons shows how directed scenes connect story, emotion, rest and rewards to play.

We examine real-time and pre-rendered cutscenes, playback speed, section navigation, full skips and quick time events (QTEs). The goal is to design both the scene and the player’s way of experiencing it.
