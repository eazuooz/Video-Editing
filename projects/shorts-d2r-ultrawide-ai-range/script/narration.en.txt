Diablo II: Resurrected. Is a wider screen always better?

In the 2021 test, the view widened to 21:9. Keep the player and monster in place. A wider view reveals a distant enemy.

But what the player can see and the monster's reaction range follow separate rules.

Players could hit those distant enemies, but the enemies did not react at that distance. You could fire; the target stayed passive.

For release, the team limited the view to 19:9. Darkened edges kept the view within the limits of the established combat rules.

Even a wider display option had to account for enemy reactions in its design.
