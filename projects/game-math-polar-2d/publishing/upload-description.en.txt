Learn polar coordinates through distance and angle. We observe gameplay, locate points, distinguish degrees and radians, handle equivalent addresses and canonical form, convert coordinates, and apply the ideas to rotation, aiming, and vector addition. Two guided exercises prepare you for the 3D continuation in part two.

This two-part polar-coordinate lecture uses narration without background music.
