Why do we need four numbers to store a rotation? We begin with imaginary numbers and planar rotation, then connect them to a quaternion describing body orientation. Keeping the same defined rotation, we examine half angles, two signs, normalization and reversal. This is the first of two lectures; the next computes ordered composition and vector rotation.
