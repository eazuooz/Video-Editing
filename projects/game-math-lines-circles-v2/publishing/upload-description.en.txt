After finding an object’s orientation in the previous lecture, how do we calculate a connection between two objects? We start with a circle as a set of equally distant points, form a line from the difference between two points, and use small numbers to separate a dimensionless fraction from travel distance. We then express a vertical line using a normal. Having built the connection, the next lecture will calculate the size of the target it may reach. This is the first of two lectures.
