The final physical part of game-rendering history episode 4. Does a more attractive image mean the underlying computation changed in the same way?

We separate reconstruction, frame generation and the appearance changes demonstrated by DLSS 5, observing surfaces in Resident Evil, Starfield, Hogwarts Legacy and basketball footage. Unreal Engine demonstrations connect exposure, tone mapping, histograms, color grading and focus controls to their inputs and results. Finally, RenderDoc events, render targets, inputs and resource budgets provide a practical inspection order.

Spatial diagrams accompany actual game and engine demonstrations. Numerical values in diagrams are illustrative. Different camera, exposure or version conditions do not prove performance or unseen implementations.
