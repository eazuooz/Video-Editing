Why Games Praise the Player | Designing Success Feedback

## 설명

A game can acknowledge a success in different ways while awarding the same score. We examine actual Nintendo Switch Sports and Ring Fit Adventure moments, then compare the timing, wording, strength, and placement of praise in an original defense prototype.

The paired screens preserve the same keyboard inputs, projectile collision rules, score, and health. We compare block/dodge-specific wording, recognition for a verified streak, honest responses to a missed attack, and placement that leaves the next threat readable. The prototype verifies controlled technical differences; subjective enjoyment still needs a human playtest.
