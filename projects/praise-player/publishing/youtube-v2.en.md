# Why Games Praise You | Success Feedback in Real Games

How does a game distinguish calculating a score from acknowledging an action the player achieved? New Hi-Fi RUSH hit, Perfect and grading scenes, and fresh Nintendo Switch Sports and Ring Fit Adventure movements and outcomes appear between chapters. Every original 2.5D explanation, defense/dodge comparison, narration and duration is preserved.

Examine timing, action-specific wording, strength, honest responses to failure and placement that leaves the next movement readable. Separate input guidance from performance feedback, and one action from an entire match result. Different commercial excerpts are examples, not equal-condition experiments or proof of player understanding. Korean narration with manually prepared English subtitles.
