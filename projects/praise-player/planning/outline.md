# Original success-recognition lesson

Viewer question: how does a game recognize what the player has actually accomplished while preserving the same rewards?

1. Fresh Switch Sports win / Ring Fit movement evaluation; distinguish calculation from recognition.
2. Prompt versus delayed praise after the same actual collision.
3. Generic versus block/dodge-specific wording from verified outcomes.
4. Small individual success versus larger verified streak; score and rules equal.
5. Deliberate miss; dishonest perfect message versus an accurate hit and acknowledgment of real partial success.
6. Same outcome/message; change overlay position and area to preserve the next threat.
