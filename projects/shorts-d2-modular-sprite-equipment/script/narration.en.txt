The original Diablo II character: redrawn for every armor combination?

The team rendered the head, torso, legs and arms, as well as the weapon and shield, separately. They turned the 3D model into component images.

Let’s separate our character the same way. Keep the other parts and replace the armor image. Now the character wears different equipment.

Use parts for the same moving pose and layer them at the matching positions. That keeps the arm and weapon moving together.

With component-based sprites, recombine the images of each part to produce multiple equipment appearances.
