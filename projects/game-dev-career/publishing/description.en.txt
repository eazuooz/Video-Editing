Who makes a game, and what does each role actually do?
This video gives a broad introduction to game designers, programmers, 2D artists, and 3D artists.

Designers shape the player experience and rules, then communicate them in a form the team can share.
Programmers make those rules and art assets work reliably inside the game.
2D artists create concepts, characters, backgrounds, UI, and the visual information players read first.
3D artists turn characters, environments, and props into spatial assets connected to movement and play.

Each field has many specializations: systems, level, and UI/UX design; gameplay, engine, graphics,
server, and tools programming; concept, character, environment, animation, and VFX art.
Names and boundaries vary by studio, project, and genre.

This is not a deep tutorial on syntax or a step-by-step production workflow.
It introduces the problems each role owns, how their results connect, and small first projects
that can help you discover which kind of work fits you.
