Does an orientation remain the same when we rewrite it as angles, a quaternion or a matrix? Following the same body’s forward and up directions, we establish coordinate and multiplication conventions, then connect conversion with reversal. Finally, we rotate and reverse a vector to check that its direction and length are restored. This is the second lecture on interpolation and rotation conversions.
