What should you separate when adding new actions to familiar controls? Observe movement, aiming and execution in actual gameplay from Anger Foot, Gunbrella and My Friend Pedro.

We distinguish button remapping from changing input devices, then examine simultaneous actions, contextual prompts and the range of target choices. Alternative input diagrams are our design comparisons, rather than evidence of these games' settings or porting results.
