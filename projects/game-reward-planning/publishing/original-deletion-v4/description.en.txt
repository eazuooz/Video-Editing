Why do we want a new character, a collectible, or a piece of music even when it does not make us stronger? From Kirby’s Copy Abilities to Smash Bros. unlocks, trophies, stickers, Spirits and music, this lesson connects each reward to what changes, how it is earned, and what the team must build.

The29-hour estimate and small checklist board are independent teaching examples, not Nintendo production data. Each gameplay clip comes from a separately credited recording.
