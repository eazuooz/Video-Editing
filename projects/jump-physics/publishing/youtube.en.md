## Description

Jumping in a game is more than simply moving a character upward. Changes to upward velocity, gravity, airtime, and air control can make the same basic action feel completely different.

In this video, we break down the fundamentals of jump physics and compare how jumping is designed in Super Mario Bros., Metroid, Ghosts 'n Goblins, Street Fighter II, Super Smash Bros., Kirby, Jump King, Celeste, and Mega Man X.

Why do some jumps feel light and floaty while others feel heavy and forceful? We will look at how button-hold duration, air control, ascent speed, and falling speed shape player decisions and overall game feel.

I hope this analysis is useful to game developers and anyone interested in character movement and game design.
