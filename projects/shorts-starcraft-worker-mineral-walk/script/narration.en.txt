These workers keep travelling between the minerals and their base. If they block one another, income stops too, so the developers added a special rule.

Normally, a unit moves around other units’ bodies. Workers meeting in a tight space can block one another and get stuck.

So while workers head to resources or return carrying them, they ignore collisions with other units.

In our demonstration, keep the units and path fixed and change only the movement state. Normal movement gets blocked; in the harvesting state, they overlap and pass through.

Order harvesting workers to stop, and they spread out to avoid overlap. This exception applies to collisions between units. It does not let them pass through walls or buildings.

The movement needed for harvesting can now keep flowing. A small exception kept the economy working without rebuilding all of pathfinding.
