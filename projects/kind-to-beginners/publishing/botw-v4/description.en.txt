Does giving a first-time player the fastest route really make the game more fun?
We look at picking up items and preparing for cold in The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild, simple interactions in Unpacking, and free exploration in A Short Hike. Align expectations, reduce the burden of the first interaction, and give players room to make the next choice themselves.
