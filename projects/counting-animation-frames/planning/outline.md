# Independent frame-timing plan

Question: how can a vague request for a faster action become a measurable edit?

1. Observe fresh Mega Man 11 / Monster Hunter Rise motion; no unverified move counts.
2. Count × reference rate: twelve steps at 60/s versus 30/s. Distinguish render and fixed-rule clocks.
3. Original input-zero intervals: startup [0,12), active [12,15), recovery [15,30). Total 0.5s at 60/s.
4. Change pose samples only; keep hit timing equal. Real input and separate collision state.
5. Original four-wall-tick hitpause freezes combat clock, not background clock; no claim of universal commercial implementation.
6. Compare render rates with the same fixed clock; observe normal speed, then verify engine logs.

Six independent scenes; gameplay/development 60% and original white 2.5D explanation 40% excluding approved intro/outro. Original Frame Timing Lab demonstrates actual controls/collision/state rather than illustrative claims. No loops or source slow-down to meet the ratio.
