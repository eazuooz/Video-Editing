The [Capcom Video Policy](https://www.capcomusa.com/video-policy/Capcom_Video_Policy.pdf), checked 2026-10-01, permits educational gameplay commentary and YouTube partner advertising. It restricts raw component distribution, standalone cutscenes, unauthorized event reposts and third-party material. We use short publisher-owned gameplay portions with original explanations; original source sound/music is omitted. This is a policy-based use record, not blanket rights clearance. Human public-rights review and actual new-video Studio checks remain pending. No paid-exclusive access is planned.

Recent title/source history and rejected candidates are recorded in `game-candidates.json`. Mega Man 11 and Rise were not found as previously used titles; the Monster Hunter franchise **was** used through Wilds in visible-rewards. Street Fighter 6/II and the latest five batch games were rejected for automatic reuse. Third-party longplays with unverified recording permission were rejected.

Fresh source SHA-256, exact IDs/owners and measured metadata are in `source-files.json`. Full downloaded info.json and media stay local and never enter Git. Final selected durations come from measured narration and `production/final-v1/plan.json`; no short footage loops or artificial slow-down. No claims that the commercial recordings reveal engine tick counts.
