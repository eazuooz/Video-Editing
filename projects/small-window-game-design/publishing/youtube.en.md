# How Screen Framing Changes a Game | Aspect Ratio and Field of View

The same game can feel different depending on what fits on screen.
What is the difference between aspect ratio, field of view (FOV), and an object's screen occupancy?

We compare 16:9 and 21:9, explore the trade-off between peripheral visibility and target size, and consider how cockpits, weapons, and UI affect readable space.
Forza Horizon 5 and DOOM are illustrative examples. The 2.5D comparisons are original diagrams, not measurements made by changing those games' camera settings.

Good screen design makes the information needed for the next decision clear.
Korean narration · Korean and English subtitles
