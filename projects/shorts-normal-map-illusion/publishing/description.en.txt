It looks like a raised brick wall, but its silhouette is flat.
Our executable Unity demonstration shows how Normal Mapping changes the directions used for lighting without changing the mesh. We compare the same wall and light, and reconstruct one frame of a 3D-character-to-2D-colour-and-normal workflow.
