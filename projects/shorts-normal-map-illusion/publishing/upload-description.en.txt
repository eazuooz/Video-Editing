It looks like a raised brick wall, but its silhouette is flat.
Our executable Unity demonstration shows how Normal Mapping changes the directions used for lighting without changing the mesh. We compare the same wall and light, show 63 sampled normals, and explain signed RGB encoding and its blue-purple neutral colour. Actual Dead Cells gameplay leads into our separate one-pose 3D-to-2D bake and relighting demonstration.
