Observe hinged doors to understand orientations between two endpoints. Following the original lesson order, compare endpoint orientation and motion history, quaternion sign selection, spherical and normalized linear interpolation, angular speed and time functions.

Actual game footage and our mathematical models are labeled separately. We do not claim to measure the game’s internal interpolation function or rotation angles. No separate background-music track was added.
